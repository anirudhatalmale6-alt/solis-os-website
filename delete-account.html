Delete Your Solis OS Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your Solis OS account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Solis OS app on your device
  2. Go to Settings (tap "More" in the bottom navigation, then "Settings")
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data including business information, customer records, appointments, invoices, and settings will be permanently removed within 30 days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • Business information and settings
  • Customer records and CRM data
  • Appointment and booking history
  • Invoices and expense records
  • Staff profiles and schedules
  • WhatsApp Assistant configuration

If you need assistance, please contact us through the app or visit solis-os.com.

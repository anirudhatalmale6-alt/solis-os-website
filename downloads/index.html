Install Solis OS POS

Set up Solis POS on iPads, Android tablets, Windows PCs, and Macs. Works on up to 8 devices at once. All data syncs automatically.

Recommended

Use Instantly in Your Browser

No download needed. Open the POS directly in your browser on any device. Works offline after first load.

Open Solis POS

Windows PC

Install as a desktop app from Chrome for the best experience.

  1. 1. Open Google Chrome and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Click the install icon in the address bar (or the 3-dot menu > "Install Solis POS")
  3. 3. Click "Install" in the popup
  4. 4. Solis POS appears on your desktop and taskbar. Works offline.
Open in Chrome

MacBook / iMac

Install as a desktop app from Chrome or Safari.

  1. 1. Open Google Chrome and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Click the install icon in the address bar (or 3-dot menu > "Install Solis POS")
  3. 3. Click "Install"
  4. 4. Find Solis POS in your Applications. Works offline.
Open in Chrome

iPad / Tablet

Add to your home screen for a full-screen app experience.

  1. 1. Open Safari (iPad) or Chrome (Android) and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Tap the Share button (iPad: square with arrow) or 3-dot menu (Android)
  3. 3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4. Solis POS appears as an app icon. Tap to open full-screen. Works offline.
Open on Tablet

Phone (Remote Dashboard)

Monitor sales and manage products from your phone while your staff uses the POS.

  1. 1. Open the POS on any device and go to Remote Access
  2. 2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera
  3. 3. Your remote dashboard opens. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4. Monitor sales, add products, and track inventory from anywhere.

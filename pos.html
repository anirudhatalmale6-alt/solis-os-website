FAQ

Common Questions

How is it so affordable compared to other POS systems?
We don't charge per-transaction fees, don't require proprietary hardware, and don't force expensive monthly plans. Our software runs on any device you already own. That means we can price it at $239/year while others charge $50–$100+ per month for similar features.
What hardware do I need?
Any tablet, iPad, laptop, or desktop with a browser. That's it. Optionally add a barcode scanner ($30+), receipt printer ($50+), and cash drawer ($30+) for a full POS station. No proprietary hardware required — use what you already have.
Does it really work without internet?
Yes. Once installed on your device, the full POS works offline. Ring up sales, manage inventory, print receipts — all without internet. When you reconnect, everything syncs across all your devices automatically.
Can I try it before paying anything?
Absolutely. You get 10 days of full access — every feature, no limitations, no credit card needed. Just sign up and start using it immediately in your browser. If you like it, subscribe. If not, no hard feelings.
Can I use it on multiple devices at once?
Yes. Run Solis POS on up to 8 devices simultaneously — iPads, Android tablets, laptops, desktops. All data (products, sales, customers, settings) syncs automatically. Perfect for multiple checkout lanes, kitchen displays, or backroom management.
Can I manage my shop remotely from my phone?
Yes! Scan the QR code in your POS settings, save it to your home screen, and you have a full remote dashboard on your phone. Add products, adjust stock, create promotions, and track sales from anywhere — at home, on vacation, wherever.
What if I need help getting set up?
We provide email support and built-in setup guides. Most people are up and running in under 5 minutes — the interface is designed to be intuitive. No technical knowledge needed.
What happens if I cancel?
You keep using Solis POS until the end of your paid period. Your data stays on your devices. No lock-in, no penalties, no hidden cancellation fees. Cancel from your account at any time.

Control Your Shop from Your Phone

Manage your entire business from anywhere. Add products, update stock, create promotions, and track sales — all from your phone. No need to be at the register.

1
Generate Your Code
In your POS, go to Settings and find Cloud Sync. Tap "Generate Code" to get your unique 6-character sync code and QR code.
2
Scan the QR Code
Open your phone camera and scan the QR code. It opens your personal dashboard instantly. You only need to scan once.
3
Save to Home Screen
iPhone: Tap the Share button in Safari, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: Tap the 3-dot menu in Chrome, then "Add to Home Screen".
4
Manage Everything
Tap the icon anytime to view sales, add products, adjust stock, create promotions, and monitor staff. Full control from your bed, your car, anywhere.

Install on Your Devices
Set up Solis POS on iPads, Android tablets, Windows PCs, and MacBooks. Works on up to 8 devices at once. All data syncs automatically.

iPad / iPhone

  1. 1. Open Safari and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Log in with your account
  3. 3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
  4. 4. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  5. 5. The app icon appears on your home screen - tap it to launch full screen

Android Tablet

  1. 1. Open Chrome and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Log in with your account
  3. 3. Tap the three dots menu (top right)
  4. 4. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  5. 5. The app icon appears on your home screen - tap it to launch full screen

Windows PC

  1. 1. Open Google Chrome and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Log in with your account
  3. 3. Click the install icon in the address bar (or 3-dot menu > "Install Solis POS")
  4. 4. Click "Install" in the popup
  5. 5. Solis POS appears on your desktop and taskbar. Works offline.

MacBook / iMac

  1. 1. Open Google Chrome and go to solis-os.com/app/
  2. 2. Log in with your account
  3. 3. Click the install icon in the address bar (or 3-dot menu > "Install Solis POS")
  4. 4. Click "Install"
  5. 5. Find Solis POS in your Applications folder. Works offline.
